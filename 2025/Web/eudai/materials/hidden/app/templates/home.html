Wallet Showcase

This demonstration shows a simplified PID issuance and selective-disclosure presentation workflow.

  • Issue a PID-like credential to your wallet.
  • Select claims to disclose and generate a presentation.
  • Send the encoded presentation to a verifier.

Getting started

  1. Register or login.
  2. Issue a credential (PID subset fields).
  3. Generate a presentation and copy the encoded payload.
  4. Paste it into the Verify page.
